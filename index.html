Welcome!

I am an Associate Professor at the Université Paris Cité working in the ALTAE linguistics lab.

My research focuses on issues in usage-based cognitive linguistics (especially Construction Grammar), sociolinguistics and English dialectology, and language variation and change, with interests in a range of empirical and quantitative methods.

This website provides a summary of my activities.

Contact: cameron.morin@u-pariscite.fr

Thank you for your visit!

Recent projects:

  • A large-scale pipeline for LLM-assisted corpus annotation (International Journal of Corpus Linguistics)
  • Personification metaphors of generative AI (AI & Society)
  • How register and region shape the language network (Constructions)
  • Sarcasm and impoliteness in interrogative constructions (Journal of Politeness Research)
  • The role of register in the contraction of gonna (English Language and Linguistics)
